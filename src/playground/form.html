폼 (Form)

텍스트 입력

<div class="form-field">
  <label for="input-text" class="form-field__label">이름</label>
  <input type="text" id="input-text" class="input" placeholder="내용을 입력하세요">
</div>

필수 입력

<div class="form-field">
  <label for="input-required" class="form-field__label">
    이메일<span class="form-field__required" aria-label="필수">*</span>
  </label>
  <input type="email" id="input-required" class="input" placeholder="내용을 입력하세요" required>
</div>

에러 상태

필수 입력 항목입니다.

<div class="form-field">
  <label for="input-error" class="form-field__label">
    이메일<span class="form-field__required" aria-label="필수">*</span>
  </label>
  <input type="email" id="input-error" class="input input--error" placeholder="내용을 입력하세요"
    required aria-invalid="true" aria-describedby="input-error-message">
  <p id="input-error-message" class="form-field__message form-field__message--error">
    필수 입력 항목입니다.
  </p>
</div>

성공 메시지

입력이 확인되었습니다.

<div class="form-field">
  <label for="input-success" class="form-field__label">이메일</label>
  <input type="email" id="input-success" class="input" value="user@example.com"
    aria-describedby="input-success-message">
  <p id="input-success-message" class="form-field__message form-field__message--success">
    입력이 확인되었습니다.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="form-field">

Textarea

최대 500자까지 입력 가능합니다.

<div class="form-field">
  <label for="textarea-demo" class="form-field__label">비고</label>
  <p id="textarea-demo-hint" class="form-field__hint">최대 500자까지 입력 가능합니다.</p>
  <textarea id="textarea-demo" class="textarea" rows="4" maxlength="500" placeholder="내용을 입력하세요"
    aria-describedby="textarea-demo-hint"></textarea>
</div>
